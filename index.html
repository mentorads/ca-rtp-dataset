7. Canadian Payment Rails: Interac vs Cards vs Crypto

Canadian casino cashier performance hinges on whether a payment method supports two-way settlement:

  • Interac e-Transfer / Interac Online: The gold standard in Canada. Deposits settle within minutes; withdrawals are routed directly back to the linked Canadian bank account once internal approval clears.
  • Credit Cards (Visa / Mastercard): Deposits often face bank-level refusal for gambling MCCs. Return-to-source limits mean withdrawals must frequently route to alternative methods.
  • Cryptocurrency (USDT / BTC): Bypasses Canadian retail banking hours entirely, offering near-instant payouts on brands like 1win and Vavada once KYC is completed.
